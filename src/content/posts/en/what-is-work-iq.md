Microsoft Work IQ allows your AI agents to access Microsoft 365 data on your behalf. With it, you can ask your agents about your emails, calendar events, Teams chats, SharePoint sites, OneDrive files, and more.

Here are some examples I use often that you can try with your agent:

- “Review my inbox, identify spam, and suggest categories for my emails. Ask me before deleting anything.”
- “What is this person talking about in email XYZ? Is there anything I need to do?”
- “Find the files related to customer X and project Y, and help me recall what we’ve already completed.”
- “Read this SharePoint site and explain what it covers.”
- “Read the messages in this Teams chat (URL) and summarize the topics discussed over the last 30 days.”

This is just a fraction of what is possible with Work IQ. Since your agent works with your permissions, it can only access information your account already has access to.
